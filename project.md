# Pintaro.ch - Professional Painting & Renovation Services Website

## Project Description
This is a static website for Pintaro.ch, a professional painting and renovation business operating in German-speaking Switzerland. The website serves as a digital presence to showcase services, build trust with potential clients, and provide contact information for inquiries.

## Business Overview
Pintaro.ch specializes in high-quality painting, plastering, and renovation services for both residential and commercial properties. The company focuses on delivering professional results with attention to detail and customer satisfaction.

## Core Services

### 1. Malerarbeiten (Painting Services)
Professional painting and coating services for interior and exterior surfaces. This includes:
- Wall and ceiling painting
- Surface preparation and priming
- Color consultation
- High-quality paint application
- Protective coatings

### 2. Gipserarbeiten (Plastering Work)
Comprehensive plastering and drywall services:
- Traditional plastering
- Smoothing and finishing
- Drywall installation and repair
- Surface preparation for painting
- Texture application

### 3. Fassaden- & Innenrenovation (Facade and Interior Renovation)
Complete renovation solutions:
- Exterior facade restoration
- Interior space transformation
- Surface repair and restoration
- Modern renovation techniques
- Quality materials and finishes

### 4. Dekorative Techniken (Decorative Techniques)
Special decorative finishes and effects:
- Venetian plaster
- Textured finishes
- Faux painting techniques
- Accent walls
- Custom decorative solutions

### 5. Schimmelsanierung (Mold Remediation)
Professional mold removal and prevention:
- Mold inspection and assessment
- Safe mold removal
- Moisture source identification
- Preventive treatments
- Long-term solutions

## Target Audience
- Homeowners seeking quality painting and renovation services
- Property managers and real estate companies
- Commercial property owners
- Architects and interior designers
- Construction companies needing subcontractors

## Website Goals
1. **Professional Presence**: Establish credibility and trust through a clean, professional design
2. **Service Showcase**: Clearly present all services with detailed descriptions
3. **Lead Generation**: Make it easy for potential clients to contact the business
4. **Local SEO**: Optimize for local searches in German-speaking Switzerland
5. **Mobile Accessibility**: Ensure excellent experience on all devices

## Key Features
- Responsive, mobile-first design
- Clear service presentation with visual examples
- Easy-to-find contact information
- Professional gallery/portfolio section
- Customer testimonials
- Request quote functionality

## Technical Implementation
The website is built using modern web technologies:
- **Next.js**: For static site generation and optimal performance
- **TypeScript**: For type-safe, maintainable code
- **Tailwind CSS**: For responsive, utility-first styling
- **Static Export**: For fast loading and easy hosting
