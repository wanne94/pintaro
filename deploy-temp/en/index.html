About Us

Pintaro GmbH has been successfully operating in the field of interior and exterior design for over two decades. Our team consists of experienced craftsmen who combine traditional techniques with modern solutions.

Our philosophy is based on three values: quality, trust and precision. With us there are no hidden costs or compromises - we execute every project with the utmost care and responsibility.

For us, every space is unique. That's why we approach individually and adapt to the wishes and needs of our customers.
